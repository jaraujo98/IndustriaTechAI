TWO VERY DIFFERENT CULTURES

Research labs and industry companies have different goals.

01

Research

Can this be done?

Does this reveal something new?

Can I demonstrate the capability?

1–10%of the work
02

Industry

Does it work reliably?

Can I install and maintain it?

Can I make money with it?

Will it still work at 3 a.m.?

90–99%of the work

Rodney Brooks had the first Roomba prototype in 1990. It hit the market in 2002.

THE MANAGEMENT CHALLENGE

How should companies keep up?

01

Focus on customer needs

Start with the outcome customers value, not the technology receiving attention.

02

Identify bottlenecks

Name the constraint that prevents that outcome today.

03

Reduce time-to-evidence

Reproduce, benchmark and decide while the information still matters.

Don't predict the future.
Be prepared for the best and the worst.
